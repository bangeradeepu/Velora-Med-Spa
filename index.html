Questions, answered

Still unsure? Call us at (331) 600-2304 and we'll walk you through it.

Is the consultation really free?

Yes. Your first consultation costs nothing and you don't have to book anything afterwards. It's time to ask questions and get an honest recommendation.

Is there any downtime?

Our treatments are non-surgical. Most clients go straight back to work or their day. Your practitioner will tell you about any short-term redness or aftercare for your specific treatment.

How many sessions will I need?

It depends on the treatment and your goals. Botox is usually a single visit, while lifting, LED and body treatments work best as a series. Your plan will spell out the number of sessions and the cost before you start.

Do you offer payment plans?

Yes. Ask about payment plans at your consultation and we'll find an option that works for your budget.
